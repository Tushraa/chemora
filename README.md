**Chemora Art** is a software development company that builds tools and software for research laboratories. We make the everyday work of a lab (keeping track of chemicals, handling invoices, analysing results, sharing research online) simpler, so researchers can spend their time on science.

This repository is the source of our company website, **https://www.chemora.art/**.

## What We Build

| Tool | What it does | Links |
|---|---|---|
| **Chemora Inventory** | Web-based chemical inventory for research laboratories. Search by name, CAS number, barcode or structure, scan and move containers, handle order requests, import and export CSV, and keep a full audit history. Several laboratories can share one platform, each with its own private data. **Free of cost for every laboratory.** | [Open the app](https://app.chemora.art/) · [Source code](https://github.com/Tushraa/chemorainventory) |
| **Invoice Management System** | Records, organises and finds purchase invoices in one place and tracks payment status, so nobody has to search through files. | [Example in use](https://harigroup.orgchem.iisc.ac.in/invoice.html) (sign-in required) |
| **LogiDFT** | Parses DFT output logs, extracts thermochemical data, formats Supporting Information and shows 3D structures and reaction energy profiles. | [Open LogiDFT](https://harigroup.orgchem.iisc.ac.in/logidft.html) |
| **Laboratory Websites** | Clean, professional websites for research groups that bring research, members, publications and the tools above together. | [Hari Group website](https://github.com/theharigroup/harigroup) |
| **Custom Tools** | Anything else a lab needs, built around its own workflow. | [booking@chemora.art](mailto:booking@chemora.art) |
